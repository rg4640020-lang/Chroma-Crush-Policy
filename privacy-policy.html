Privacy Policy - Chroma Crush

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Chroma Crush ("the App") is a free puzzle game published by Pocketjoy Games, operated by Rahul B. Gaikwad, an individual based in Mumbai, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the App does with information. It describes the App as it works today.

Short version

The App has no accounts, no sign-in and no online features. Your progress stays on your own device. The App does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Information stored on your device

  • Game progress and settings. Levels completed, stars, coins, boosters, special pieces, rank, theme and sound choices, and similar game data are saved only on your device. If you uninstall the App or clear its data, this progress is removed.
  • Difficulty tuning. The App measures how you play (for example, wins, fails and speed) on your device to adjust level difficulty to you. This stays on your device.
  • Your privacy choice. The App remembers your answer to its privacy prompt. You can change it at any time with the PRIVACY button on the home screen.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising ID or any other personal information. The App contains no advertising and no third-party analytics code that sends data off your device.

Advertising and purchases

This version of the App shows no ads and has no in-app purchases. If ads or optional purchases are added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the App will ask for your consent where the law requires it.

Virtual items

Coins, stars and boosters are earned by playing. They have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for real money, goods or prizes.

Children

The App is a general puzzle game with no social features and no personal-data collection. It is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Security

Because the App keeps its data on your device and does not transmit it, your information is protected by your device's own security.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the App or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Nothing is held on our servers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.
